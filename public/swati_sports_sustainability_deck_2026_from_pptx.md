BATTERY RECYCLING
Pyrometallurgical Processing
It uses very high temperatures to melt & separate metals from spent lithium-ion batteries. It’s fast & widely used but consumes a lot of energy & usually can’t recover lithium effectively.
SWATI's Recycling Plan
Partner with EV OEMs and fleet operators for end-of-life battery collection
01
Establish pre-processing facility for dismantling, sorting & black mass production
02

License hydrometallurgical technology for high-purity material recovery

03
Hydrometallurgical Processing
Supply recovered Li, Co, Ni back to domestic battery manufacturers
Uses chemical solutions (acids, bases, or solvents) to dissolve & extract metal ions from crushed battery material. It gives high recovery & purity of metals like Li, Co, Ni, and Mn with lower energy use.
04
